Privacy Policy

What We Collect

Noor collects only what is necessary. When you create an account we collect your email and display name. We store Quran progress, lesson completions, and dhikr session logs. Guest mode sends nothing to our servers. We do not collect location data and do not track behavior for advertising.

What We Never Do

We never sell user data to any third party. We never share data outside of what is required to operate the service. We never show ads inside the app. We never use your data to train AI models without explicit consent.

Data Storage

User data is stored in Supabase on AWS. Every user's data is isolated using Row Level Security. One user cannot read another user's data. Connections are encrypted with TLS.

Your Rights

You can request a copy of all data we hold. You can delete your account and all data from Settings. You can use Guest mode with nothing leaving your device. A child's progress is never deleted without parent confirmation.

Terms of Service

Using Noor

Noor is provided by Kulan Group Ltd. By using the app you agree to these terms. You must be 18 or older to create an account. Parents may create child profiles for children in their care.

Subscriptions

Noor Family and Noor Education are subscription tiers billed through Apple or Google. Free trials require no credit card. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Refunds follow App Store or Google Play policies.
